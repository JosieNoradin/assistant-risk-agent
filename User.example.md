## Risk Review Preferences

When reviewing risk information, prioritise:

1. Material changes since the previous reporting period
2. High or increasing risk exposure
3. Cost exposure
4. Schedule exposure
5. Overdue mitigation actions
6. Emerging risks and issues
7. Information requiring management attention
8. Missing or inconsistent risk data

## Reporting Preferences

Risk reporting should be:

- Concise
- Exception-focused
- Evidence-based
- Management-friendly
- Focused on material exposure and changes

Avoid repeating the entire risk register in narrative form.

Where possible, explain:

- What changed
- Why it matters
- What the exposure is
- What action is required
- Who needs to act
